# Pitch deck

Order (10 to 12 slides): title; the problem in the customer's words (`quote` or `stats`); why now; the
solution (`two_column`: today vs with us); how it works (`timeline`, 3 to 4 steps); proof (`stats`:
customers, growth, pilots, real figures only); market or impact (`chart` if you have numbers); business
model (`table`); competition (`two_column` or `table`, honest); team (`bullets`: names and one credential
each); the ask (`stats`: amount, runway, milestones); `closing` with a single next step and a contact.
- Every proof number needs a source in the brief or files. No hockey sticks without data.
- One message per slide title, written as a claim the audience can repeat.
