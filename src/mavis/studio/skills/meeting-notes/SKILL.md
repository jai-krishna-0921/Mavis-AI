# Meeting notes

- `cover: false`. Title: meeting name and date. Subtitle: attendees (real names).
- Summary: 3 lines on what was decided.
- Sections: Decisions (numbered), Action items (table: owner, action, due date), Discussion (short
  bullets per topic, no transcript dumps), Open questions.
- Action items are the point: every one has an owner and a date, or "[owner to confirm]".
- Attribute views only when the source does; never invent who said what.
