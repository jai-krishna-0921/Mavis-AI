# Status update

Lead with the answer: overall status (on track, at risk, off track) and why, in one line.
- Deck order: title; `stats` (3 to 4 headline numbers vs target); done since last update (`bullets`);
  `timeline` of what is next with dates; risks and blockers with owner (`table`: risk, impact, owner,
  action); decisions needed (`bullets`, phrased as questions with a deadline); `closing`.
- Doc: summary with status and decisions needed first; then progress, metrics table, risks table, next
  steps table (owner, action, date).
- Compare against the last update or the plan whenever the brief has the numbers. Red news early, plainly.
