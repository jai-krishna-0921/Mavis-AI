# Personalisation

The user's profile and brand come with the request. Use them:
- Company, team and role: put the right names on the title slide, cover and closing (presenter, team,
  "Prepared for"), and pitch the content at their level.
- Their register: match how they write (formal or casual, British or American spelling, Indian number
  grouping such as 18,00,000 and "lakh" when they use it).
- Their people and projects: use the real names, project names and dates you were given; never generic
  placeholders like "John Doe" or "Company X".
- Style: when a brand style is set, the renderer applies it; do not fight it in the copy. When none is set,
  choose the style that fits the purpose and audience: `corporate` for clients, finance and leadership;
  `bold` for pitches and launches; `minimal` for internal updates; `warm` for people, culture and
  education; `editorial` for long-form reports and strategy.
