# Proposal

Structure: summary (the ask, cost, timeline, outcome in 4 lines); the problem and its cost today; the
proposed approach (phases with what each delivers); scope (in and out, as a two-column table or two lists);
timeline (table: phase, dates, deliverable); cost (table with total); risks and mitigations; why us or
why now; next steps with a decision date.
- Name the reader and their organisation; write to them ("you"), not about them.
- Every cost and date from the brief or marked "[to confirm]".
